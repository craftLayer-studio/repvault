# 06 Go-to-market and onboarding: RepVault

## Positioning statement

For independent gym and studio owners in India who lose money to missed renewals and members who quietly stop coming, RepVault is a QR-and-WhatsApp member engine that collects renewals on time and flags at-risk members before they quit. Unlike record-keeping gym apps, RepVault is built to grow revenue; unlike global tools, it costs under ₹2,000 a month and works on WhatsApp and UPI.

## Sales script (field visit, 5 minutes)

1. **Opener:** "Sir, quick question: how many members' renewals were due last month, and how many paid on time?" (Most owners do not know. That is the hook.)
2. **Pain:** "Most gyms lose 10–20% of renewals just because nobody followed up on time. And new members who come less than 4 times in the first month usually quit." (Cite Gymdesk benchmark if asked.)
3. **Show:** Phone demo: scan the QR, see status, show the WhatsApp reminder with UPI link, show the at-risk list.
4. **Math:** "At your fee of ₹1,200, two saved renewals pay for RepVault. Everything after that is profit."
5. **Close:** "Let's put the QR up today. 14 days free. We import your members for you. If renewals don't improve, don't pay."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I already use WhatsApp and a register." | "Keep WhatsApp. RepVault sends from WhatsApp for you, on time, with a payment link, and shows who has not paid. The register cannot tell you who is about to quit." |
| "Too expensive, another app is ₹500." | "That app stores names. RepVault collects money. If it does not recover more than ₹1,999 in 30 days, cancel." |
| "My members won't scan." | "Put the tablet at the desk; staff scans the member's QR in 2 seconds. Most gyms see 60%+ scanning in a month with a 'scan to enter' rule." (Validate with pilots.) |
| "I have a fingerprint machine." | "Keep it for the door if you like. QR adds renewals and churn alerts, and you stop storing fingerprints, which is sensitive data under the new DPDP rules." |
| "No time to set up." | "We set it up. Send a photo of your register; we import members today." |
| "Is my data safe? Will you sell my members?" | "Your members are yours. We never message them except on your behalf. Export or delete any time. DPDP-compliant consent is built in." |

## Activation milestones

- Day 2: QR installed, ≥20 check-ins.
- Day 7: ≥40% of active members checked in; first renewal paid via link.
- Day 30: ≥60% members checked in; owner opened at-risk list ≥10 days.

## Retention playbook (keeping gyms as our customers)

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Check-ins drop below 30% of members for a week | Call owner; re-train desk; suggest "scan to enter" rule |
| Owner has not opened dashboard in 7 days | Daily WhatsApp summary already sends numbers; add a "money recovered this month" line |
| Plan nearing member limit | Upsell prompt with exact members over limit |
| Annual renewal of our plan due in 30 days | Send "RepVault year in review": renewals collected, members rescued, leads converted |

## Upsell paths

1. Starter to Growth when the gym passes 150 members or asks for lead follow-up (trigger: member count).
2. Growth to Pro when the gym runs a challenge or opens a second branch.
3. WhatsApp marketing credits at festivals (New Year, Diwali, summer body season).
4. Turnstile integration once QR adoption exceeds 70%.
5. Cross-sell CraftLayer products: GlowDesk to gyms with salon/spa corners; CareQueue to gyms with physio.
