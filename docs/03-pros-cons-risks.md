# 03 Pros, cons, risks: RepVault

## Pros

| Pro | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Large, growing, fragmented market | About 46,500 commercial fitness centres in India in 2024, market expected to grow about 15% a year to 2030 ([Deloitte/HFA](https://www.deloitte.com/in/en/about/press-room/indias-fitness-market-to-double-by-2030-per-a-deloitte-and-hfa-report.html), accessed 2026-11-24) |
| Clear ROI story | Two saved renewals a month cover the subscription |
| Low hardware dependence | QR plus phone or tablet; installs in one visit |
| Recurring, sticky data | Attendance and payment history make switching painful |
| Reuses core platform | QR, CRM, payments, WhatsApp, reviews already in core; faster MVP |
| Natural upsells | PT, classes, campaigns, turnstiles, multi-branch |

## Cons

| Con | Impact |
|---|---|
| Crowded category | Dozens of Indian gym tools on Capterra India and Techjockey, many from ₹809 per month ([Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/directory/33276/gym-management/software), accessed 2026-11-24) |
| Price-sensitive buyers | Small gyms compare against "free" Excel and WhatsApp |
| Owner time is scarce | Onboarding must be done for them, not by them |
| Churn of small gyms themselves | Some gyms close; our logo churn includes business failures |
| WhatsApp cost and policy dependence | Meta controls pricing and template approval |

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Members do not scan (low activation) | Medium | High | Desk tablet mode; "no scan, no entry" policy suggested; streak rewards for scanning |
| WhatsApp marketing price rises (marketing rate rose about 10% on 1 Jan 2026 per [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline), accessed 2026-11-24) | High | Medium | Keep reminders as utility templates; sell marketing as credits; respond inside free 24-hour service window |
| Template rejection or number ban for spammy sends | Medium | High | Opt-in only, frequency caps, quality monitoring, owner cannot send untemplated bulk |
| DPDP non-compliance (main obligations from about May 2027) | Medium | High | Consent records, purpose limits, opt-out, minors flow, data deletion built in MVP |
| Competitor price war (₹500/month tools) | High | Medium | Compete on outcomes (renewals, churn rescue), not features list; annual plans |
| Founder over-builds features | High | High | Strict P0 list; ship in 8 weeks; feature requests need 3 paying gyms asking |
| Field sales cost too high for small gyms | Medium | High | Cluster sales by locality; referral programme; WhatsApp demo videos |
| GST rule ambiguity on memberships | Low | Low | Configurable GST per plan; CA review |

## Kill criteria

Stop or pivot RepVault if any of these hold at the checkpoints below.

| Checkpoint | Kill / pivot trigger |
|---|---|
| Week 8 (pilot end) | Fewer than 2 of 3 pilot gyms willing to pay at any tier |
| Day 60 after MVP | Fewer than 10 paying gyms after 100+ owner conversations |
| Month 4 | Activation below 40% (members with a check-in in first 30 days) across paying gyms |
| Month 6 | Monthly logo churn above 6%, or blended ARPU below ₹1,200 |
| Month 6 | CAC payback above 9 months on actual numbers |
| Month 9 | MRR below ₹1 lakh with no channel showing improving CAC |

Pivot options if killed: sell only the churn-and-renewal module as a WhatsApp add-on to existing gym software users; or fold check-in into a CraftLayer product with better traction (ClassNest for studios).
