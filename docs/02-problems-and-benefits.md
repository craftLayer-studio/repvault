# 02 Problems and benefits: RepVault

Format: problem, then RepVault solution, then benefit and the metric it moves.

## Owner

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renewals lapse because follow-up is manual and late. Vendors report that WhatsApp follow-ups break once a gym gets busy ([Innoviti](https://innoviti.com/blogs/how-gyms-and-fitness-studios-can-reduce-payment-failures-and-revenue-leakage/), accessed 2026-11-24) | Automated T-7/T-3/T-0 WhatsApp reminders with UPI link; T+3 desk task | More renewals collected on time, less awkward chasing | Revenue, retention |
| Early drop-off: 45% of new members cancel within 90 days in Wodify's pre-pandemic data; Gymdesk's 2026 benchmark puts it near 1 in 5 ([BarBend](https://barbend.com/wodify-blog-series-reveals-45-of-members-quit-after-90-days); [Gymdesk](https://gymdesk.com/blog/gym-retention-rate-benchmarks), accessed 2026-11-24) | Daily at-risk list from attendance rules; one-tap personal WhatsApp; trainer call tasks | Rescue members before they mentally quit | Retention |
| Walk-ins leave without giving details, or details sit in a notebook | Trial QR captures phone with consent and auto-follows up for 7 days | More trials, structured follow-up | Acquisition |
| No idea of real numbers: active vs expired, dues, peak hours | Live dashboard and daily WhatsApp summary | Decisions on staffing, pricing, offers | Revenue |
| Trainer commission disputes | PT packs with member sign-off per session and commission report | Fewer disputes, PT revenue grows | ARPU |
| Fingerprint machines break and store biometric data | QR check-in on phones or a ₹8,000 tablet (estimate) | No hardware lock-in, lower DPDP exposure | Activation, risk |
| Few Google reviews vs chain gyms | Review request after 10th check-in to happy members | Better local search ranking | Acquisition |

## Staff (front desk, trainers)

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper register and manual expiry checks | Scan shows status instantly | Check-in in seconds, no arguments | Activation |
| Chasing dues face-to-face is awkward | System sends the reminder; desk only handles exceptions | Less conflict, fewer missed dues | Revenue |
| Trainers do not know which members are slipping | At-risk list assigned to trainers | Trainers keep their own clients, earn PT | Retention, ARPU |
| Writing diet plans again for every member | Templates assigned in one tap | Time saved, consistent quality | Retention |

## Member (end customer)

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forgets renewal date | Timely WhatsApp with one-tap UPI payment | No gap in membership | Retention |
| Wants proof of payment and GST invoice | Auto invoice on WhatsApp | Trust, reimbursement possible | Retention |
| Loses motivation after week 3 | Streaks, challenges, check-in milestones, trainer nudge | Keeps habit going | Retention |
| Hates installing apps | Everything via WhatsApp links and a PWA | Zero friction | Activation |
| Worried about data misuse | Clear notice, opt-out link in every marketing message, no biometrics | Control over own data | Trust |

## Walk-in / prospect

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wants to try before paying, dislikes pushy sales | Self-serve free trial pass via QR | Low-pressure trial | Acquisition |
| Does not know prices | Plans shown on gym's public page after scan | Transparent decision | Acquisition |

## Money summary for the owner (example, estimate)

A 300-member gym at ₹1,200 per month average fee collects about ₹3.6 lakh per month (estimate). If RepVault lifts on-time renewals by 10 points and rescues 5 at-risk members a month, that is roughly 30 + 5 = 35 member-months, or about ₹42,000 per month of retained revenue (estimate). The Growth plan costs ₹1,999. That is the core sales argument: **"RepVault pays for itself if it saves two renewals a month."**
